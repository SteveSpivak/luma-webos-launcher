- This advanced launcher is a **sibling fork**, not a direct descendant or compatible patch target of older `SteveSpivak/lg-luma`. NEVER apply `delivery/patches/focus-depth.patch` to this repo. Source-reuse data under `lab/references` are historical. Advanced launcher already has its own motion, widgets and video backend. Do not transplant a second set.
- Advanced launcher uses `com.denisefremov.luma`; the existing `com.ecklf.luma` is a **different** app. For TV test we must assign a distinct *candidate app ID*, including any player/companion/storage paths that are bound to the old ID, before installation. Do not rename now without dependency and rollback audit.
- Nuvio `appinfo.json` declares `space.nuvio.webos`, `handlesRelaunch: true`. Historical `launchDeepLink.js` consumes `params.contentTarget`; it may defer until auth/profile is ready. Lab-only `launchIntent.js` (keyboard) is **NOT** the production bridge.
- A deep link to a title opens **details**, not proof of streaming playback or first frame. Respect `streamAutoPlayMode=MANUAL`; do not infer different app behavior across version/profile/cloud config.
- A profile-aware CW feed, custom poster rows, and recommendations are different data contracts. Separate each, preserve user privacy, enforce expiration, cancellation, offline behavior, bounds and provenance.
- Root-only Home takeover, `tv/home.sh`, `tv/home.cjs`, native Starfish/player/film planes, `native/smp/`, firmware, DRM, network DNS and permanent configuration are out of scope. No TV or other device contact during this task.
